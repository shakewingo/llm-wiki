# Applied AI and Evaluation Map

Applied AI is where assumptions meet messy data, user consequences, and operational
metrics.

[LLM evaluation](../concepts/llm-evaluation.md) provides the cross-cutting discipline.
For observability, study [time-series anomaly detection](../concepts/time-series-anomaly-detection.md),
[SR-CNN](../concepts/spectral-residual-cnn.md), and
[VAE–LOF](../concepts/vae-lof-anomaly-detection.md). Then distinguish association
from causality through [causal structure learning](../concepts/causal-structure-learning.md),
[PC](../concepts/pc-algorithm.md), and [GES](../concepts/greedy-equivalence-search.md),
before combining evidence in [AI-assisted RCA](../concepts/root-cause-analysis.md).

[Multimodal systems](../concepts/multimodal-ai-systems.md) demonstrate pipeline-level
error compounding, and [synthetic personas](../concepts/responsible-synthetic-personas.md)
show why consent and identity safety are product requirements.

Practice with [applied-AI drills](../interview/applied-ai-drills.md).
