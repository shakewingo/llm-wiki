# Responsible Synthetic Personas

A synthetic persona combines generated dialogue, voice, or appearance to simulate a
specific identity, creating unusually high consent and deception risks.

Responsible design repeatedly discloses that the system is simulated, obtains valid
consent for source media, restricts impersonation and redistribution, protects stored
biometric material, and marks generated output where appropriate. Memorial systems
also require grief-sensitive limits and easy disengagement. Technical quality cannot
substitute for authorization, psychological safety, legal review, or abuse controls.
